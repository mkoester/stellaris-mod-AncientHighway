# Lore (to be worked out)

Status 2026-11-03: a stub — the starting point and the questions it raises.

## Starting point

- The ring is artificial, built a long time ago by a space empire that is lost and forgotten.
- Its systems have no ordinary star in the centre but *something artificial* — what, is open.
- Its connections to the regular galaxy decayed; studying them is how an empire learns of the
  builders and how to repair them.

## Open questions

- Who were the builders, and why build a road around the core rather than through it?
- What sits in the centre of a ring system instead of a star — and does it still do something
  (power, a beacon, a lock)?
- Why did it decay, and why does nobody remember it? Do the Fallen Empires know, and what do they
  think of younger empires reopening it?
- Is it guarded (a guardian, a dormant defence), or simply abandoned?
- How does the lore fit the game's existing precursors and the L-Cluster's gates — related, or
  deliberately separate?
- What do the study events reveal, step by step?
- Implications for gameplay: anything the lore promises (e.g. a guardian, an awakening) must exist
  in the mod.
