# Ancient Highway

A Stellaris mod idea (4.5.x, working title, not playable yet): a ring of artificial, unclaimable
systems around the galactic core — built long ago by a forgotten empire — that every empire can
reach once it has studied and repaired an entry point. Inspired by the super highways of X4.

*In other 4X games you settle on land but can still sail almost anywhere across the oceans. In
Stellaris there is no ocean: after a few decades most empires are landlocked, and an ally at war on
the far side of the galaxy is beyond help. The Ancient Highway is that ocean.*

The repo holds design notes only so far. See `AGENTS.md` for the status, the feasibility findings
and the plan, `docs/design.md` for the mechanics and `docs/lore.md` for the (unfinished) lore.

Unofficial fan project, not affiliated with Paradox Interactive.
